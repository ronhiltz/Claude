How to play Wizard

60 cards: the 52 standard cards plus 4 Wizards and 4 Jesters.

Rounds

  • Round 1 deals 1 card each, round 2 deals 2, and so on.
  • After dealing, the next card is flipped to set trump. A flipped Wizard lets the dealer choose trump; a flipped Jester means no trump. If the deck is empty, there's no trump.

Bidding

  • Starting left of the dealer, everyone bids exactly how many tricks they will take.
  • Bids do not have to add up to the number of tricks — unless the "hook" rule is on, which forbids the dealer from making them balance.

Playing

  • You must follow the led suit if you can. Wizards and Jesters may always be played.
  • If a Wizard is led, no suit is established — anyone may play anything.
  • Jesters led set no suit; the first real card after them does.

Winning a trick

  • The first Wizard played wins, always.
  • Otherwise the highest trump wins.
  • Otherwise the highest card of the led suit wins.
  • All Jesters? The first one played wins.

Scoring

  • Bid exactly right: +20, plus 10 per trick taken.
  • Off by any amount: −10 for each trick over or under.
  • Highest total after the last round wins.
